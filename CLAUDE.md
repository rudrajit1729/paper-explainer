# paper-explainer

This repo makes animated explainer videos from research papers. For any request about making,
editing, restyling, narrating, translating or exporting a video, load the skill in
`.claude/skills/paper-explainer/SKILL.md` and follow it.

- Projects live in `projects/<name>/`. Never edit `build/`.
- Look at stills (`explainer still`) before rendering; use `--preview` before full quality.
- Keep numbers, quotes, names and titles exactly as the paper states them.
- Writing style for on-screen text and narration: short declarative sentences, active voice, no em dashes.
