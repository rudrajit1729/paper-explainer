To Boldly Go: What has AI let developers unlock?

Animated explainer

Most studies ask how much faster AI makes developers.

We asked what it lets them do that they couldn’t do before.

N = 331 developers at Microsoft

Developers and AI are joint learners.
Developers learn with AI. AI learns from what developers codify.
Each turn of the loop, both zones move outward.
At the nexus, AI supplies skill faster than it can judge. Developers bridge the gap.
When skill outpaces judgment, the loop stalls.

A two-way playbook.

Scaffolding runs in both directions, at every point in the work.
Before acting
Demonstration
Developer → AI

Pair each rule with work that meets it and work that almost does.

AI → Developer

Show how an expert would approach the problem.

While acting
Articulation
Developer → AI

Have AI state its goal and assumptions, then walk through its work.

AI → Developer

Think aloud with AI, then compare your approach with its alternatives.

When it goes wrong
Marking the discrepancy
Developer → AI

Judge AI’s work with tests, evals, or a different model.

AI → Developer

Point to the error, not the fix.

After acting
Transfer of responsibility
Developer → AI

Hand over routine work end to end. Keep the decisions.

AI → Developer

Take on the specialist’s work and build the judgment to own it.
